Who We Are

About ARISE

ARISE Marketing Intelligence is a strategic knowledge platform built on the book "Marketing Intelligence: The Complete Digital Playbook" by MD ABIDUR RAHMAN, published in Dhaka, Bangladesh, 2026.

MD ABIDUR RAHMAN is a practitioner-level digital marketer and author based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with hands-on experience across local and global markets. His 17-chapter playbook distils real-world campaigns, costly lessons, and proven frameworks into a single coherent guide — so you don’t have to wander between hundreds of courses and blog posts.

The complete audiobook is available free on YouTube — read it, listen to it, or both. The knowledge doesn’t change: understand buyer psychology, master channel economics, and build lasting brand trust.

Three of the practitioner principles from Marketing Intelligence that consistently change how founders approach growth.

"Trust is the only asset that cannot be copied by competitors, appreciates with age, and reduces every cost of acquisition. Every brand decision should be filtered through one question: does this build or erode trust?"
