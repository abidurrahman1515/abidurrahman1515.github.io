About the Author

MD ABIDUR RAHMAN

📍 Dhaka, Bangladesh — 2026

MD ABIDUR RAHMAN is a practitioner-level digital marketer and the author of Marketing Intelligence: The Complete Digital Playbook. With hands-on experience across markets in Dhaka and internationally, he has worked with early-stage startups through to established B2B organisations, running campaigns spanning SEO, paid media, email automation, and brand strategy.

His 17-chapter handbook synthesises frameworks from HubSpot, Google, Ahrefs, Cialdini, and dozens of additional authoritative sources into a single coherent guide designed for founders and practitioners who need clarity without an agency budget.
