Technical SEO Audit Checklist: 25 Steps to Fix Your Site Architecture

  8. Key Takeaways

Your website could have the best content in the world, but if search engines can't properly crawl, render, and index it, you will never rank. Technical SEO is the foundation beneath every other SEO strategy — and without it, you are building on sand.

This 25-point technical SEO audit checklist will help you systematically identify and fix the issues preventing your site from reaching its full organic potential.

Why Technical SEO Is Non-Negotiable

Google's crawlers are sophisticated but not infallible. Poorly structured websites, slow page speeds, and broken internal links create friction that costs you rankings — and money. A comprehensive technical audit is typically the highest-ROI activity for sites that already have solid content but are underperforming in search.

Stat: Pages that load in 1 second have 3x higher conversion rates than pages that load in 5 seconds (Portent, 2023). Speed is both an SEO and revenue lever.

1. Crawlability & Indexation (Steps 1–6)

  • Step 1: Check your robots.txt file. Ensure you are not accidentally blocking important pages from being crawled.
  • Step 2: Submit and verify your XML sitemap in Google Search Console. Ensure it is up to date and contains only canonical, indexable URLs.
  • Step 3: Audit noindex tags. Check that no important pages have accidental noindex directives.
  • Step 4: Use Google Search Console's Coverage report to identify crawl errors, 404s, and soft 404s.
  • Step 5: Check your crawl budget efficiency using a tool like Screaming Frog — identify orphaned pages and redirect chains.
  • Step 6: Verify that your site is accessible to Googlebot by checking for JavaScript rendering issues.

2. Site Speed & Core Web Vitals (Steps 7–12)

Core Web Vitals (CWV) are Google's official page experience signals. They consist of three metrics:

  • Largest Contentful Paint (LCP): Time for the largest visible element to load. Target: <2.5 seconds.
  • Interaction to Next Paint (INP): Responsiveness to user interactions. Target: <200ms.
  • Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS): Visual stability. Target: <0.1.
  • Step 7: Run your site through PageSpeed Insights and address the top recommendations.
  • Step 8: Implement lazy loading for below-the-fold images.
  • Step 9: Compress and convert images to WebP format.
  • Step 10: Eliminate render-blocking resources (unnecessary CSS/JS in the <head>).
  • Step 11: Enable server-side caching and use a CDN for static assets.
  • Step 12: Set explicit width and height attributes on all images to prevent CLS.

3. Structured Data (Steps 13–15)

  • Step 13: Implement Article or BlogPosting schema on all blog posts.
  • Step 14: Add Organization and WebSite schema to your homepage.
  • Step 15: Validate all structured data using Google's Rich Results Test tool.

4. Canonicalization & Duplicate Content (Steps 16–18)

  • Step 16: Ensure every page has a self-referencing canonical tag.
  • Step 17: Implement 301 redirects for any legacy or duplicate URLs (e.g., HTTP to HTTPS, www to non-www).
  • Step 18: Audit for thin or duplicate content using tools like Siteliner or Screaming Frog.

5. Mobile Optimization (Steps 19–21)

  • Step 19: Verify mobile-friendliness in Google Search Console's Mobile Usability report.
  • Step 20: Ensure tap targets (buttons, links) are at least 48x48px and properly spaced.
  • Step 21: Check that your viewport meta tag is correctly set: content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0".

6. Internal Linking Architecture (Steps 22–25)

A strong internal linking structure distributes PageRank efficiently and helps Google understand your site's topical authority.

  • Step 22: Ensure your most important pages (pillar content) receive the most internal links.
  • Step 23: Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text for internal links.
  • Step 24: Fix all broken internal links (404 errors).
  • Step 25: Eliminate redirect chains in internal links — link directly to the final destination URL.

Key Takeaways

  • Technical SEO is the foundation — all other SEO work depends on it.
  • Core Web Vitals directly impact rankings; prioritise LCP, INP, and CLS.
  • Structured data increases click-through rates from search results pages.
  • Audit your site regularly — technical issues can appear as your site grows.
  • Use internal linking strategically to signal pillar pages to Google.
